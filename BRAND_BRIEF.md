# Cadence — Brand Brief

## Core Idea

Cadence is about **steady, thoughtful progress** in product development.  
It stands for rhythm, discipline, and long-term thinking, the opposite of chaotic shipping or flashy velocity.

Cadence builds mobile software with care, clarity, professional restraint, and a sustainable working pace.

The brand expresses confidence through **calmness rather than noise**.

---

## Purpose

To help organisations turn ideas into **reliable, well-designed mobile applications**, delivered carefully, responsibly, and with craft.

Cadence isn't selling speed. It's selling trust, quality of execution, and continuity over time.

---

## Positioning

A **senior, craft-led mobile product practice** operating between design studio and engineering partner.

Clients choose Cadence when they want a thoughtful builder rather than an agency factory, maturity over bravado, dependable delivery and maintainability.

Tone of relationship: **collaborative, calm, pragmatic, expert**.

---

## Essence (Brand DNA)

Three core qualities:

**Rhythm — Clarity — Dependability**

Supporting traits: disciplined, measured, trustworthy, intentional, understated, technically grounded.

Never loud. Never whimsical. Never frantic.

---

## What Cadence Believes

Good products are understood before they are built, shaped with care, iterated deliberately, and maintained properly.

Good process creates good outcomes.

Sustainable pace > heroics.  
Depth of craft > superficial polish.

---

## Visual & Expression Direction

The brand should feel geometric, minimal, precise, and confident without theatrics.

Identity themes:

- rhythm through spacing, not imagery
- movement implied, not illustrated
- structure rather than metaphor

### Colour Palette

Keep layouts **minimal, spacious, and disciplined**. Avoid decorative elements and gradients. Use colour sparingly and with intention.

Target balance: **90–95% neutral tones, 5–10% accent**

The palette draws subtle inspiration from the ochre landscapes of Provence, grounding Cadence in place without literal reference. These are not decorative colours. They are structural.

**Primary Colours:**

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| **Charcoal** | `#2D2926` | Text, logo, primary UI elements. Warmer than pure black, more grounded. |
| **Bone** | `#F7F4EF` | Page backgrounds, content blocks. A warm off-white, like limestone. |

**Accent:**

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| **Ochre** | `#C2571A` | Primary CTAs, link hover states, key emphasis markers. |

The ochre is derived from the burnt sienna tones of Roussillon's quarries. It reads as confident and distinctive without the synthetic brightness of typical tech oranges.

**Secondary (optional, use sparingly):**

| Colour | Hex | Usage |
|--------|-----|-------|
| **Stone** | `#E8D5B7` | Section differentiation, subtle backgrounds where Bone needs contrast. |
| **Slate** | `#5E7A8C` | Secondary text, disabled states, supplementary information. A cool counterweight. |

Use Ochre only for:

- primary call-to-action buttons
- link hover or active states
- key emphasis or markers

Do **not**:

- use Ochre for large backgrounds or section fills
- introduce additional accent colours
- tint, soften, or apply gradients to the accent

### Typography

Font: Inter, Avenir Next, or Neue Haas Grotesk

Modern, human, professional. Use generous spacing and line height. Prefer **Regular / Medium** weights (400/500). Avoid rounded or condensed fonts. Keep headings calm, not shouty or heavy.

Tone should feel **measured and professional**.

### Components & Layout

**Buttons:**

- Default: Charcoal text on Bone
- Primary emphasis: Ochre fill with white text

**Dividers & Rules:**

- Thin Charcoal lines (not grey, not pure black)

**Sections & Cards:**

- Flat backgrounds (Bone or Stone)
- No shadows, gradients, or soft UI

**Icons & Graphics:**

- Simple, geometric, minimal
- Charcoal or Slate only

### Imagery

Prefer product screenshots, diagrams, and typography-led layouts.

Avoid stock photos, illustrations, and lifestyle imagery.

If using photography, choose neutral, minimal compositions. Architectural or landscape imagery from the Luberon region may be appropriate in limited, considered contexts, but should never dominate.

### Implementation Principle

Ask: *"Does this feel clear, intentional, and disciplined?"*

If something feels decorative, noisy, or energetic, simplify it.

Cadence communicates identity through **restraint, rhythm, confidence, and craft**, not through colour, decoration, or visual noise.

---

## Voice & Messaging Tone

The voice is calm, thoughtful, honest, direct, and free of hype.

Language should communicate competence through **clarity, not persuasion**.

Avoid buzzwords, startup slogans, and energetic marketing language.

Prefer plain, precise statements, grounded explanations, and authenticity.

---

## Who Cadence Is For

Organisations who value quality over speed, longevity over novelty, and considered progress over churn.

Typical partners: SMEs, mission-led organisations, established teams evolving their product, and clients who appreciate dialogue and thinking.

---

## What Cadence Is Not

Cadence is not a fast-turnaround dev shop, a trendy creative agency, a startup-style growth lab, or a "move fast and break things" outfit.

It doesn't sell intensity. It sells **confidence and stability**.

---

## Summary Statement

Cadence is a mobile product practice built on rhythm, clarity, and dependable craft.  
It helps organisations move from ideas to stable, maintainable software, at a sustainable pace, with care and intention.
